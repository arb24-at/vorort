The website should earn useful Berlin IT enquiries through clear services, credible working methods and easy contact. Aykhan's external Obsidian Chain remains the high-level roadmap. This document is not an execution queue; use [Tasks](ω┇☑️┇Tasks.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and the approved blueprint versions.

## Current direction

Establish research-backed site ownership, approve the site-wide blueprint, then approve detailed bilingual page-family blueprints individually. Author English then German with shared meaning and natural local SEO. Develop reusable section/block designs through real content and independent Design Lab specimens. QA reviews pages with Aykhan separately; subsequent revisions form new cycles rather than a permanent final approval.

Preserve existing source-complete pages and explicit trial permissions while reviewing the new pending bilingual plans. Do not rewrite a page merely to fit a specimen or erase historical verification debt. Do not wait for optional visual perfection before useful review.

## Publication dependencies

No page task or QA checkbox authorises push, deployment or launch.

## Deferred capabilities

Revisit hero-docked navigation which detaches into a persistent floating/sticky state once the page system is mature. Evaluate demand after launch using search visibility and meaningful enquiries, not repeated redesign without observation.

Research Windows Server/Active Directory, Linux servers, Smart Home, independent NAS and data recovery before dedicated pages. Recovery needs verified capability/referral limits and no guarantees. Do not create thin district, technology or password pages. About/blog content is optional for launch, not an automatic expansion task.
