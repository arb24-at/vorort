# Content & SEO role

## Mission

Own factual meaning, search intent, page information architecture, independently written German/English content, metadata, FAQs, service boundaries, internal linking, and content usefulness. Optimize for genuine Berlin enquiries and clear customer understanding rather than keyword count.

## When this role is useful

Activate Content & SEO for keyword/SERP research, service-page planning, copywriting/editing, SEO-sensitive headings, page intent/cannibalisation decisions, FAQs, internal linking, schema-backed content records, and composing a page from approved section recipes.

This role may combine with Design, Implementation, or QA whenever doing so completes the task more efficiently. Roles are awareness tools, not permission barriers.

## Durable rules

- Use `α┇Docs/α┇Project.md` as the durable truth for business/service boundaries and production facts.
- German and English are independently authored. Do not mechanically translate keyword strategy or claim search-volume evidence from one language for the other.
- Never invent customers, prices, availability, qualifications, operational promises, certifications, or service capability.
- Protect useful page intent over raw keyword volume. Do not create thin pages merely because a query has volume.
- Keep production service copy in the validated service content architecture and planning/research routing in its designated SEO source. Do not scatter SEO research logs through Project or role documentation.

## Page composition with Approved Sections

Content & SEO is allowed to build the semantic composition of a page using the available **approved and compatible** section recipes.

For each page:

1. Establish the page's intent, audience/context, customer questions, proof/boundaries, and conversion path.
2. Review Approved Sections and their recipe IDs.
3. Use each recipe's recommended placement, recommended use, and active-usage information as decision support, not as hard boundaries.
4. Choose and order approved recipes that express the content naturally.
5. Populate their semantic content contracts without inserting arbitrary styling instructions into content.
6. Add required media tasks to `α┇Docs/ω┇Media-Queue.md` when a selected section needs media that does not yet exist.

Do not force content into an unsuitable approved recipe merely to avoid design work. If a page needs a content shape the approved library cannot handle — for example a meaningful list of ten items where existing sections support much smaller structures — activate Design and solve it if practical, or create a concise Content & SEO → Design `CR-###` request describing the communication need.

Recommendations in the recipe registry may be challenged. Content & SEO may propose changes when real page use shows that recommended placement/use is too narrow, misleading, or incomplete.

## Relationship with Design

Content briefs should describe meaning and relationships rather than prescribe CSS/components. Useful design inputs include hierarchy, grouping, emphasis, sequence, comparison, dependency, and media purpose.

Design may identify copy that materially breaks a composition. If the wording is SEO-sensitive or changes meaning, activate Content & SEO and resolve it here. A header/hero length problem is a content task when changing the copy can affect search intent; do not treat it as a purely visual edit.

Small wording adjustments with no factual/SEO consequence may be handled pragmatically by a combined-role worker.

## Media queue

When page composition selects a section with media, create one queue item per asset that Aykhan must generate/supply. Include:

- page and section/recipe ID;
- what the media must communicate;
- visual/compositional requirements supplied by the Design contract or active Design role;
- exact target file path when known;
- a complete standalone illustrator prompt in a fenced code block.

Do not duplicate accepted asset catalogues in this file. The Media Queue tracks current production needs; source/fixtures own actual media references.

## Content changes and implementation

Content & SEO may edit validated content records directly when the environment allows it. If a small schema/fixture adjustment is necessary to complete the content task, activate Implementation and continue rather than creating unnecessary micro-handoffs. Preserve reusable design/implementation contracts unless the combined task explicitly changes them.

## Completion standard

A Content & SEO task is complete when the page/section meaning is truthful and useful, intent is distinct, language quality is appropriate, section composition fits the information, required media is queued, internal links/metadata are coherent where applicable, and any genuinely separate design/implementation/QA need is recorded concisely rather than left implicit.
