# α · Research & SEO

## Purpose and inputs

Establish what people need, which real page should answer, and how strong the evidence is. Read AGENTS, Project service boundaries, the relevant site/page blueprint and the matching entries in `ω┇Engine/src/data/seo-keywords.yml`. Inspect existing Keyword Planner evidence before proposing more research.

## Method

1. Identify the audience, actual service capability, route/translation ID and adjacent-page ownership.
2. Audit existing evidence in each locale. Preserve Aykhan's Keyword Planner findings, source links, dates, exclusions and valid volumes. Do not label all research missing because some entries are provisional.
3. Investigate genuine gaps with focused English/German SERP and intent review; consult primary technical sources when service explanations need checking. Record what was searched, when and what it supports. A language sanity check is not Keyword Planner volume evidence.
4. Distinguish local service intent from shopping, device instructions, provider faults, unsupported repairs or enterprise expectations. Prefer useful truthful coverage over a larger-volume misleading term.
5. Update the designated SEO record, retaining its schema and honest `researchStatus`. Provisional seeds remain provisional until evidence supports an upgrade. Never invent volumes, ranking estimates or translation-derived research.

## Handoff to Blueprint Architect

Provide a short brief: primary intent per locale, supporting topics, excluded intents, distinct page ownership, evidence level and unresolved questions. Reference the canonical SEO IDs rather than duplicating research logs in blueprints. English and German share planned service meaning; exact keywords and natural wording differ. Research does not approve a blueprint or authorise new services.

## Completion

Evidence is accessible, locale status is accurate and the next worker can identify what is established versus uncertain. Leave specific remaining research questions in the blueprint/handoff. Use AGENTS for Drive readback and unavailable-verification rules.
