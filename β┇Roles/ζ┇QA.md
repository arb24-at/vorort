# ζ · QA

## Purpose and inputs

Review the source-complete page with Aykhan in a separate QA task. Read AGENTS, the approved blueprint/version, changed sources, impact notes and relevant Collaboration RVs. A technical pass is not blueprint, recipe or publication approval. Pages remain open to future revision cycles.

## Review dimensions

| Area | Evidence to gather |
| --- | --- |
| Meaning and editorial quality | Planned information in both locales, natural headings/prose, truthful scope, no filler/repetition or invented facts |
| SEO and relationships | Actual evidence status, owned intent, metadata, canonical/alternate pairing, base-aware real links/assets |
| Reader journey | Useful recognition/scope before early enquiry, easy direct contact, coherent optional depth and planned return path; no fixed universal section count |
| Shared contracts | Known compatible recipes/blocks, independent lifecycle, exact page permissions, unique IDs, valid targets and affected-consumer preservation |
| Responsive/accessibility | Representative desktop/mobile hierarchy, crops, tables/overflow, source order, focus/keyboard, labels, reduced motion and readable contact dock |
| Contact | One enquiry, truthful transport, required/conditional fields, disabled inactive fields, disclosure, privacy/sensitive-data notice, fallbacks and honest status |
| Design Lab | Independent generic specimens, parent/block labels, Proposed unimplemented ideas, derived usage/Approved view, no review chrome in production |

Use actual rendered evidence for visual/interactive claims. Inspect affected routes and variants proportionately; do not repeat full-site/browser checks without a new risk. Capture screenshots only when useful. With shell access, run applicable validation, `git diff --check` and status; verify representative unchanged consumers for shared changes. For delivery, test a configured destination before claiming receipt.

## RV handling and findings

Drive workers may complete content/source with `RV-###` work still pending. Validate that existing change first. Do not redesign or rewrite unless a real failure requires it; add the needed role for a bounded fix. Consolidated RVs retain their IDs and unresolved obligations until actually checked. State source/repository/browser/Git/deployment evidence separately using AGENTS definitions.

Record findings with affected route/source, consequence, evidence and next action. Distinguish a defect from a Product Owner choice or missing business fact. Keep software's older disclosure form, artwork fit and provisional SEO status visible until reviewed. Backend/privacy and public identity dependencies cannot be passed by aesthetic inspection.

## Completion

Check the assigned QA task only after its defined checks and review have happened. If some evidence is unavailable, leave the task open or explicitly scope completed subchecks without implying a full pass. Remove resolved active RV obligations and retain a concise result; future improvements get new tasks. No permanent final-page approval or automatic deployment follows QA.
